PMPeak: free product management course for beginners

PMPeak teaches product management from scratch in 9 chapters, completely free, with no sign-up. Every lesson uses plain English, step-by-step diagrams and real product case studies, and every chapter has quizzes. Practise with mock product manager interviews.

What you will learn

  1. Mapping business outcome to product outcome: systems thinking, vision and strategy, KPI trees, discovery, design thinking
  2. Mapping outcomes to opportunities and user research: jobs-to-be-done, personas, journey maps, research process
  3. Problem framing, ideation and validation: hypotheses, ideation, mind maps
  4. Metrics and analysis: metrics, A/B testing, analysis
  5. Driving product growth: growth, positioning, growth loops
  6. Designing and building a prototype: design, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, SQL basics
  7. Deep diving into data: cohort and funnel analysis, SQL for product managers
  8. Machine learning foundations and generative AI: ML, LLMs, prompt engineering
  9. Fundamentals of tech and system design: APIs, application layer, building systems
