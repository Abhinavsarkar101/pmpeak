PMPeak: free product management course for beginners

PMPeak teaches product management from scratch in 16 weeks, completely free, with no sign-up. Every lesson uses plain English, diagrams and real product examples, and every chapter has quizzes. Finish with mock product manager interviews.

What you will learn

  1. Mapping business outcomes to product outcomes
  2. Product discovery and user research
  3. Problem framing, ideation and prioritisation
  4. UX design and AI-assisted prototyping
  5. Product growth, metrics and analytics
  6. Tech 101 and system design
  7. Machine learning, LLMs and prompt engineering
  8. RAG, fine-tuning and AI workflows
  9. AI agents and agentic systems
  10. AI evaluation and monitoring
  11. PM interviews: guesstimates and root cause analysis
  12. PM interviews: product design and metrics
  13. PM interviews: strategy, go-to-market and AI product sense
  14. Weeks 14 to 16: revision, mock interviews and final assessment
